### What's fixed

#### Bottom controls are no longer under the navigation bar

The app paints its own background from edge to edge — Android 15 asks that of every app that
targets it, and KScanner has always done it — but we laid out the bars that carried the bottom
controls as if the system's navigation bar were not there. On a phone with three on-screen buttons
the bar covered the lower part of those controls and took their taps: press the shutter and Home
answered, press **Export PDF** and nothing happened.

Measured on a 1080x2400 phone with three-button navigation, where the system bar occupies the last
135 px: in the document screen **Add page** and **Export PDF** were drawn at 2276–2333, entirely
inside the bar; in the editor the straighten row ran to 2338. Both are now above it, and the camera's
shutter — which ended 56 px inside the bar at 2321 — is clear as well. The same fault put the
camera's top row under the clock and the screen's cutout; that strip is fixed the same way. The
bars still paint their colour to the bottom of the window, so nothing shifted colour behind the
system buttons.

The navigation bar was not the cause and is not hidden. It stays where the system puts it, and what
moved is only where our own controls start.

### Also in this release

- **Nothing else changes.** Every control does what it did: the same shot, the same crop, the same
  filters, the same automatic shutter and the same framing outline. No setting appears or
  disappears. What changed is where the buttons are drawn, not what they do.
- The library, the settings list and the page viewer needed nothing: their content and their
  floating buttons already sat inside the space the system bar leaves, which is why the fault
  looked like a few screens misbehaving rather than the app having no scheme at all.

### Known issues

- **The live outline is found on a subsampled frame.** The preview's grayscale image is made small
  for detection by skipping pixels rather than averaging them, so the detector sees a slightly more
  aliased picture here than it does on a photograph you import. On a busy background that can cost
  the live border confidence that a still shot keeps.
- **A border can run off the screen.** The preview fills the screen by cropping the frame, so when
  the page reaches the frame's sides — which is what happens when it fills your view — the part of
  the border tracing those sides is drawn outside the visible area. It marks where the page really
  is, but on a tall phone it can read as two lines rather than a frame.
- Folder support is unreachable end to end. No code assigns a document to a folder — every
  document is created with a null folder — so the folder filter never appears at all.
- Rename works on one document at a time: with more than one selected the Rename action is not
  offered at all. Bulk delete is correct.
- In the editor, **Detect** discards a manual rotation: re-running detection resets the corners
  to the detected quad.
- Rotating a page does not invalidate the stored OCR line boxes, so text selection on a rotated
  page can still point at the pre-rotation positions.
- `sweepCaptures`, the cleanup of stale capture files, is written but not called from anywhere.
